What is deliberately not here

Flared gas volume and CO2 are not computed. That conversion needs a calibrated combustion efficiency and heating value this project has not established. An uncalibrated figure under a regulatory heading is a fabricated measurement with legal consequences, so the column is absent rather than estimated. A test rejects any field named co2, carbon, volume or tonne.

How to read this

Sites are identified by OSM polygon, not by name. Thousands of polygons share the placeholder “Unnamed Industrial Site”, and grouping on the name attributed 94,088 detections from across the country to a single row — one operator’s emissions credited to another.
